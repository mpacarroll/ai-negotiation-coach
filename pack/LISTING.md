## Description

> You know you should negotiate. That was never the problem.
>
> The problem is the moment they say "that's above our band" and your mind goes blank, or they
> ask what you currently earn and you answer before you have thought about it, or you hear
> yourself say "but I'm flexible" two seconds after naming your number.
>
> This is 41 scripts for those moments. Word for word, short enough to say out loud, organized
> by the situation you are actually in.
>
> **What is inside**
>
> - **Salary and raises, 14 scripts.** The counter. What to say when they ask your expectations
>   first. What to say when the offer is "final". How to ask for a raise without ambushing your
>   manager. What to ask for when the cash genuinely will not move.
> - **Rent, 7 scripts.** The renewal ask that works because it happens before they set the
>   number. How to say the vacancy math out loud without it sounding like a threat.
> - **Bills and subscriptions, 6 scripts.** The one word that gets you to the department that
>   can actually discount things, and why the first offer they make is never their best.
> - **The hard moments, 9 scripts.** You already named a number and it was too low. They went
>   silent for a week. They gave you a deadline of today. You have no walk-away and you know it.
> - **In writing, 5 templates.** Counter by email, the raise request, and the one-paragraph
>   summary after a verbal yes that stops it evaporating.
> - **A ten-minute prep** to do the night before, and **four rehearsal prompts** that make any
>   AI assistant argue back harder than the real thing.
>
> **What it is not.** Not a book about negotiation theory. Not 200 pages. Not a course. You can
> read the section you need in ten minutes and go into the conversation with the words in your
> head.

## FAQ, for the listing

**Is this just the free tool in a PDF?**
No. The free tool builds your plan and gives you five general lines. This is the 41 specific
scripts for what happens after the plan meets a real person, including the situations the tool
cannot cover.

**How long is it?**
20 pages. Deliberately. It is a reference you use, not a book you finish.

**Does it work outside the US?**
The scripts do. One note on pay history is US-specific and says so.

**What if I have already messed up the negotiation?**
Script 28 is exactly that, and it is the single most used page in the pack.
